Herend Gifts for Every Occasion

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

The easiest Herend gift to choose is one tied to an occasion: a porcelain baby shoe or a First Curl Box for a new baby, Wedding Bells for a wedding, a Graduation Owl for a graduate, an angel or a cross for a faith milestone, and a small box or a Golden Edge coaster as a thank-you.

New baby and christening

The Herend baby shoe is one shape painted in many patterns. At Paris Tableware it comes in Chinese Bouquet Green, Blue, Multicolor, Rust and Raspberry, plus Rothschild Bird, Golden Edge, Blue Garland, Printemps and Royal Garden Flowers. A thoughtful choice is the pattern the family already sets the table with; the Herend Chinese Bouquet pieces at Paris Tableware show that pattern's colors.

For keepsakes with a job to do, the Herend First Curl Box has a small fishnet bear on the lid, and the Herend Tooth Fairy Box has a fishnet bunny. The Herend Baby Bottle Figurine and Herend Baby Rattle Figurine suit a nursery shelf. See them all in Herend children's gifts.

Names and initials in porcelain

Herend makes every letter of the alphabet as its own figurine, so a row of letters can spell a child's name or a couple's initials on a mantel. On October 24, 2026, all 26 letters were in stock at Paris Tableware. Most come in gold fishnet, like the Herend Letter M Figurine, while Herend Letter O Figurine and Herend Letter X Figurine come in a range of colors. Browse the Herend alphabet letters.

Weddings and anniversaries

The Herend Wedding Bells Figurine shows two white bells with gold rims tied with a fishnet bow, and we carry it in several colors. For an anniversary, the Herend Kissing Swans Figurine and Herend Cuddling Bunnies Figurine are both pairs.

For a couple starting a dinner service, Herend Golden Edge is a natural first pattern: white porcelain with a gold rim. Its plain white center lets a colorful pattern join it later. Our store's wedding registry guide explains how to set up a registry.

Graduation and teachers

The Herend Graduation Owl Figurine wears a mortarboard and glasses and comes in nine fishnet colors, so you can match school colors. The Herend Pair of Owls on Book Figurine works for a teacher or a pair of graduates. More owls are in our Herend owl guide.

Faith milestones and sympathy

The Herend Guardian Angel Figurine shows an angel guiding a child. The Herend Miniature Cross Figurine comes in several colors, and the Herend Star of David Figurine is also available. See all Herend religious figurines.

Hostess and thank-you gifts

Small porcelain boxes are easy to wrap and easy to place on a dresser or a desk. The Herend Chinese Bouquet Blue Ring Box and Herend Golden Edge Ring Box are easy choices, and the Herend Golden Edge Coaster, given in multiples, suits anyone who entertains. See the full range of Herend boxes.

Holidays

For December, our Herend Christmas guide covers the nativity, snowmen and holiday china. For Easter, bunnies are the largest animal group in our Herend stock; start with the Herend bunny guide.

Common questions

What is a Herend baby shoe?

A small porcelain shoe kept as a baby keepsake. Herend paints the same shoe shape in many patterns; Paris Tableware carries it in Chinese Bouquet colors, Rothschild Bird, Golden Edge, Blue Garland, Printemps and Royal Garden Flowers.

What Herend gift suits a wedding?

The Herend Wedding Bells Figurine is made for the occasion. For a couple starting a table, a first Golden Edge place setting is a gift they can add to over the years.

Does Herend make every letter of the alphabet?

Yes. Each letter is its own figurine, and on October 24, 2026 all 26 were in stock at Paris Tableware, most in gold fishnet.

Can I ship a Herend gift directly to the recipient?

Yes. Enter the recipient's address at checkout. Paris Tableware ships within the continental US, free on orders over $150.

Herend at Christmas: Nativity, Snowmen and Holiday China

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

Most of Herend's Christmas range is figurines: a nativity you can build one figure at a time, snowmen, nutcrackers, Christmas trees and a ball-shaped ornament, most of them in several fishnet colors. For the table, Herend also paints holiday china, including the Golden Laurel and Green Laurel patterns and a Christmas dessert series of plates and mugs.

Building a Herend nativity piece by piece

The Herend nativity is sold as separate figures, so you can start small and add to it. Begin with the Herend Nativity Scene Figurine, which shows the Holy Family under a stable roof. Then add:

  • The three kings: Herend Caspar Figurine, Herend Melchior Figurine and Herend Balthazar Figurine.
  • Shepherds: Herend Shepherd with Lamb Figurine and Herend Shepherd Boy and Lambs Figurine.
  • Animals: Herend Donkey Figurine, Herend Nativity Lying Cow Figurine and Herend Wandering Camel Figurine.
  • Angels: Herend Nativity Angel Figurine and Herend Kneeling Angel Figurine.

In our catalog these nativity figures share one decor code, BETH1, so they are painted to go together even when you buy them years apart. See them together in the Herend nativity collection.

Snowmen, nutcrackers and Christmas trees

The Herend Snowman Couple Figurine pairs two snowmen in contrasting colors, such as black and key lime. Other snowmen include the Herend Mini Snowman Figurine, Herend Candy Cane Snowman Figurine, Herend Skiing Snowman Figurine and Herend Sledding Snowman Figurine, most of them in several fishnet colors. Browse Herend snowmen.

Herend's nutcrackers include the Herend Small Nutcracker W/Candy Cane Figurine, Herend Nutcracker with Sword Figurine, Herend Nutcracker Drummer Figurine and Herend Mouse King Nutcracker Figurine. For trees, there are two sizes, the Herend Christmas Tree Figurine, 4.75" high and the Herend Christmas Tree Figurine, 9" high, plus the Herend Christmas Tree Bunny Figurine and the ball-shaped Herend Christmas Ornament Figurine. Putting Christmas figurines away after the season

Dust each piece before it goes back in its box, wrap it on its own in soft tissue, and pad the thin parts, such as a snowman's hat or an angel's wings. Our figurine care guide has more on packing and display.

Common questions

Can I buy a Herend nativity one piece at a time?

Yes. The Herend Nativity Scene Figurine, the three kings, shepherds, animals and angels are sold separately, and the nativity figures share one decor code so later additions match.

Which Herend snowmen and nutcrackers are there?

Among others, the Herend Snowman Couple Figurine, Herend Mini Snowman Figurine, Herend Candy Cane Snowman Figurine, Herend Skiing Snowman Figurine and Herend Sledding Snowman Figurine, most in several fishnet colors, and nutcrackers such as the Herend Nutcracker Drummer Figurine and Herend Mouse King Nutcracker Figurine.

Does Herend make Christmas china?

Yes. Golden Laurel and Green Laurel are full holiday dinner services, Winter Shimmer adds holly and gold dots, and the Christmas dessert series has plates and mugs with a tree, gingerbread, a snowman or a Santa bunny.

When should I order Herend for Christmas?

Order early if a piece is meant for a particular day. If you need it by a specific date, email info@paristableware.com before you order and we will tell you what is realistic.

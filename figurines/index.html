Herend Animal Figurines: Colors, Codes and Where to Start

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

A Herend figurine is a small porcelain animal made and painted by hand at the Herend manufactory in Herend, Hungary. Most are covered in fishnet, a fine net of painted scales finished with real gold. Each model comes in one or more colors, and its item number tells you exactly which model and color you have.

What fishnet means

Fishnet is the fine painted net of scales that covers most Herend animals, drawn in one color and finished with touches of gold. Herend's own website lists these pieces as scale variations and shows its realistically painted animals separately, as naturalistic figurines. The fishnet color is part of each piece's item number, which makes a color easy to look up.

Fishnet, two-color or Reserve: the styles you will see

Most figurines fall into one of three styles. The photo usually tells you which, and the item number confirms it.

  • Single-color fishnet. The whole animal is netted in one color. Of the 749 Herend figurines we had in stock on October 24, 2026, 486 were offered in more than one color. The Herend Owl Miniature Figurine, for example, comes in Raspberry, Green, Black, Blue and Rust.
  • Two- or three-color fishnet. Pairs and groups are often painted in contrasting colors, with names such as Blue-Raspberry, as on the Herend Kissing Swans Figurine.
  • Reserve Collection, single decoration. Almost every Reserve model is made in one decoration only. Most are painted like the real animal, as on the Herend Wood Duck Figurine; a few, such as the Herend Proud Peacock Figurine, pair a fishnet body with painted plumage. Of our 749 figurines, 95 carry the Reserve tag.

A newer group sits in between: a white animal with fishnet only on an accessory, such as the sweater on the Herend Toy Poodle Figurine or the party hat on the Herend Party Elephant Figurine. On these models the color name describes the accessory.

How to read a Herend figurine item number

Take VHB---05102-0-00, the Herend Owl Miniature Figurine in Blue:

  • VHB is the decor, Blue fishnet. Hyphens fill the decor out to six characters.
  • 05102 is the five-digit model number. It stays the same in every color.
  • -0-00 is the ending most figurines share.

To match a piece you own, note the model number: the same owl in Raspberry is VHP---05102-0-00. One model number can also carry two versions: the Herend Large Rabbit Figurine is VHB---05334-0-00, and the Herend Rabbit with Applied Flowers Figurine, a Reserve piece, is VHS13305334-0-92. Sizes: miniature, standard and large

Herend puts the size in the name when a model is unusually small or large: on October 24, 2026 we had 21 models with Miniature in the name and 14 with Large. The Herend Miniature Elephant Figurine stands 1.75 inches, the Herend Young Elephant Figurine 3.75 inches and the Herend Large Elephant Figurine 9.5 inches. Half of the fishnet models without a size word stand between 2 and 3.25 inches.

Three ways to start a collection

  • By theme. Garden animals, the pets in your life, safari animals, or a holiday shelf.
  • By life event. One figurine for each birth, graduation or wedding. Our Herend gift guide has ideas.
  • One model, several colors. The same small model in different colors, such as the Herend Owl Miniature Figurine in five, makes a calm, ordered row.

Caring for Herend figurines

Dust with a soft cloth, skip lemon-based products and abrasives, and hold a figurine by its body. Our figurine care guide covers display, packing and what to do if a piece breaks.

Common questions

Are Herend figurines hand painted?

Yes. Herend describes assembling its figures by hand from several molded parts, painting them with squirrel-hair brushes and finishing them with real gold, followed by a decor firing at about 800 degrees Celsius. The painting is done at the manufactory in Herend, Hungary.

Why do two Herend figurines of the same model look slightly different?

Because each one is painted by hand, the spacing of the net, the gold touches and the eyes vary a little from piece to piece. To match a figurine you already own, start from the item number on its base: the same five-digit model number and decor code give the closest match.

Does Herend retire figurine colors and models?

We don't have a published retirement list to share, and stock changes color by color. If one color matters, email info@paristableware.com and we will check.

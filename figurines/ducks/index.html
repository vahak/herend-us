Herend Duck Figurines

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

Herend ducks come in two families. Fishnet ducklings such as the Herend Duckling in Boots Figurine are small, playful and sold in several colors. Naturalistic waterfowl from the Reserve Collection, such as the Herend Wood Duck Figurine and the Herend Common Loon Figurine, are larger and made in one decoration. Paris Tableware had 12 duck models in stock on October 24, 2026.

The Sitting Duck and Standing Duck pair

The Herend Sitting Duck Figurine and the Herend Standing Duck Figurine have consecutive model numbers, 16313 and 16314. On October 24, 2026 both were in stock in the same seven colors: Blue, Sapphire, Butterscotch, Raspberry, Black, Green and Key Lime. In both, the duck is white and the color is a fishnet ribbon tied at the neck. They are sold separately, so you can match the ribbons or mix two colors. One sits 2 inches high; the other stands 3.25 inches.

Choosing a Herend duck as a gift

Ducklings suit a baby shower or a first birthday; the Herend Rubber Ducky Figurine is an easy nod to bath time, and the Herend Party Duckling Figurine wears its own party hat. The naturalistic waterfowl suit someone who fishes, hunts or keeps a lake house. For more ideas by occasion, see our Herend gift guide.

Caring for a duck figurine

Lift a duck by its body rather than its bill or tail. Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. Our care and replacement guide has more.

Common questions

Are the Herend Sitting Duck and Standing Duck sold as a pair?

No, they are sold separately. They have consecutive model numbers, 16313 and 16314, and on October 24, 2026 both were offered in the same seven colors, so they are easy to pair.

What is the difference between Herend ducklings and the Reserve ducks?

The ducklings are small fishnet pieces, mostly 1.5 to 3.25 inches tall, often offered in several colors. The Reserve Collection ducks, such as the Herend Wood Duck Figurine and Herend Mallard Duck Family Figurine, are painted like the real birds, made in one decoration and measure 9 inches long or more.

How big is the Herend Mallard Duck Family Figurine?

It measures 11.25 inches long and 8.75 inches wide, as listed on the Paris Tableware product page.

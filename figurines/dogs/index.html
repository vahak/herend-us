Choosing a color for a Herend dog

Black, Blue and Butterscotch are offered on the most dog models: 41, 39 and 36 of the 54 we had in stock on October 24, 2026. Raspberry and Chocolate follow, on 31 and 30. Two simple ways to choose:

  • Match the coat. Butterscotch for a yellow Lab or a golden, Chocolate for a chocolate Lab, Black for a black dog, Rust for a red Dachshund.
  • Match the room. Blue and Sapphire sit well beside blue-and-white china, and Key Lime or Green suits a garden room.

Some newer models leave the dog white and put the fishnet on a garment. The Herend Toy Poodle Figurine wears a fishnet sweater and the Herend West Highland Terrier Figurine a fishnet coat, so the color you pick is the color of the clothing.

Gifts for a new puppy, or in memory of a dog

For a new puppy, the playful models fit best: the Herend Puppy in Crate Figurine, the Herend Frisbee Dog Figurine and the Herend Dog with Ball Figurine. For a family remembering a dog, a quiet portrait of the breed in Black, Blue or the dog's own coat color is a gentle choice. If you are not sure which model is closest to a particular dog, email info@paristableware.com with the breed.

Sizes and care

Most Herend dogs are small: the Herend Labrador Retriever Figurine stands 3.25 inches, the Herend Dachshund Figurine 2.25 inches high and 3.25 inches long, and the Herend Miniature Puppy Figurine 1.5 inches. Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. Our care and replacement guide has more.

Common questions

Does Herend make a figurine of my dog's breed?

Check the breed table on this page, which lists the 21 breeds we had in stock on October 24, 2026. If yours is missing, email the breed to info@paristableware.com and we will check what Herend currently offers.

Do Herend dog figurines come in natural coat colors?

Not among the 54 dog models we had in stock on October 24, 2026. Nearly all are painted in fishnet, and the few exceptions carry a decorative pattern rather than a coat, such as the white-and-gold Herend Foo Dog Looking Right Figurine or the Chrysanthemum version of the Herend Seated Dog Figurine. The closest match is a fishnet color near your dog's coat, such as Butterscotch for a yellow Lab or golden, Chocolate for a chocolate Lab, or Black.

How big are Herend dog figurines?

Most are small. The Herend Labrador Retriever Figurine stands 3.25 inches, the Herend Dachshund Figurine 2.25 inches high and 3.25 inches long, and the Herend Miniature Puppy Figurine 1.5 inches, as listed on the product pages.

Trunk up: the good-luck elephant

Many gift buyers look for an elephant with its trunk raised, a popular good-luck symbol. That is a folk belief, not something we have seen Herend state. If it matters to you, these models show a raised trunk in our photos: the Herend Elephant Luck Figurine, Herend Joyful Elephant Figurine, Herend Elephant with Tusks Figurine, Herend Young Elephant Figurine, Herend Party Elephant Figurine and Herend Reach For the Stars Figurine. The Herend Little Elephant Figurine, Herend Miniature Elephant Figurine and Herend Large Elephant Figurine carry the trunk low.

Which colors do Herend elephants come in?

Blue is offered on 11 of the 16 elephant models and Raspberry on 9. Key Lime, Black and Rust follow, on 6 models each. A few models add a mixed palette listed as Multicolor, including the Herend Elephant Luck Figurine and the Herend Joyful Elephant Figurine. On the Herend Party Elephant Figurine the elephant itself is white, and the color you choose is its fishnet party hat.

  • A new baby. The Herend Baby Elephant Figurine, or for a larger gift the Herend Mother and Baby Elephant Figurine, an adult and calf side by side, 10.5 inches long.

Caring for an elephant figurine

Lift an elephant by its body, never by the trunk or tusks. Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. Our care and replacement guide has the details.

Common questions

Does a raised trunk on a Herend elephant mean good luck?

A raised trunk is a popular good-luck symbol. That is a folk belief, not something we have seen Herend state. If you want one, six models show the trunk raised: the Herend Elephant Luck Figurine, Herend Joyful Elephant Figurine, Herend Elephant with Tusks Figurine, Herend Young Elephant Figurine, Herend Party Elephant Figurine and Herend Reach For the Stars Figurine.

What is the smallest Herend elephant?

The Herend Little Elephant Figurine, 1.25 inches high and 2.25 inches long, followed by the Herend Miniature Baby Elephant Figurine at 1.5 inches and the Herend Miniature Elephant Figurine at 1.75 inches.

What is the largest Herend elephant?

Of the models we had on October 24, 2026, the Herend Large Elephant Figurine is the tallest at 9.5 inches, and the Herend Elephant Figurine from the Reserve Collection is the longest at 12.25 inches.

Herend Bird Figurines, from Songbirds to Peacocks

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

Herend bird figurines come in two looks. Fishnet birds such as the Herend Bird with Nest Figurine are small and sold in several colors. Naturalistic birds from the Herend Reserve Collection, such as the Herend Eastern Bluebird on House Figurine, are painted like the real species and made in one decoration. Of the 61 birds we had in stock on October 24, 2026, 22 are Reserve pieces.

Herend peacock figurines

We had two Herend peacocks in stock on October 24, 2026, both Reserve pieces made in one decoration. The Herend Proud Peacock Figurine pairs a blue fishnet neck and body with painted wings and a long green tail lowered behind it, 9 inches long and 6 inches high. Herend swan figurines

The Herend Kissing Swans Figurine sets two swans face to face so their necks form a heart. It comes in Blue-Raspberry, Blue and Raspberry-Key Lime, measures 6.5 inches long, and suits a wedding or an anniversary. Single swans come in two sizes: the Herend Swan Figurine, 4" long, in Raspberry, Black or Blue fishnet, and the larger Herend Swan Figurine, 6.75" long, a Reserve Collection piece made in one decoration: white, with fine gray fishnet on the neck and wings and an orange bill. All three are in the store's Herend swan collection.

Handling a porcelain bird

Beaks, tail tips and branch ends are the slimmest parts of a bird figurine, so lift it by the body or the base. Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. See our care and replacement guide for more.

Common questions

What is the Herend Reserve Collection?

At Paris Tableware, Reserve Collection is the tag on Herend pieces that are almost always made in a single decoration, most of them painted to look like the real animal; a few, such as the Herend Proud Peacock Figurine, pair a fishnet body with painted plumage. We had 106 Reserve pieces in stock on October 24, 2026: 95 figurines, 22 of them birds, plus a few vases, jars and dishes.

How should I handle a porcelain bird figurine?

Lift it by the body or the base, never by the beak, tail or a branch, since those are the slimmest parts. Dust with a soft cloth and skip abrasive or lemon-based products.

Does Herend make parrot and hummingbird figurines?

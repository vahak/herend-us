Herend Cat and Kitten Figurines

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

Herend's cats are mostly playful kittens painted in fishnet. On October 24, 2026, Paris Tableware had 20 Herend cat models in stock, and every one except the Herend Cat Nap Figurine came in more than one color. Blue, Raspberry and Black are offered most. The easiest way to choose is by pose.

Herend cat figurines by pose

Most Herend cats are small, between 1 and 2.75 inches tall, so several fit on one shelf or a desk.

Which colors do Herend cats come in?

Blue is offered on 15 of the 20 cat models, Raspberry on 12 and Black on 11. Rust and Butterscotch follow, on 9 and 7. Pairs come in two-color combinations such as Green-Blue or Raspberry-Key Lime.

Two models break from the all-over net. The Herend Miniature Cat Figurine is offered in Natural as well as Blue, Black and Raspberry. On the Herend Sweater Kitty Figurine the kitten is painted in natural colors, and the color you choose is its fishnet sweater.

  • For a move: the Herend Cat in Box Figurine.

Caring for a cat figurine

Ears and tails are the fine points on a porcelain cat, so pick it up by the body. Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. Our care and replacement guide covers the rest.

Common questions

Is there a Herend cat in natural colors?

Yes. The Herend Miniature Cat Figurine is offered in Natural as well as Blue, Black and Raspberry, and the Herend Sweater Kitty Figurine shows a naturally painted kitten wearing a fishnet sweater.

What is the smallest Herend cat figurine?

The Herend Miniature Kitten Figurine, 1 inch high and 1.5 inches long, followed by the Herend Miniature Cat Figurine at 1.5 inches.

Which Herend cat makes a good gift for a book lover?

The Herend Cat on Books Figurine shows a kitten settled on a stack of books. It stands 2.75 inches high and was offered in nine colors on October 24, 2026.

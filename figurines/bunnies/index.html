Herend Bunny Figurines: How to Choose One

Last updated October 24, 2026. Written by Tamar Medzadourian, Paris Tableware, Ridgewood, New Jersey.

Herend makes more bunnies than any other animal we carry. On October 24, 2026, Paris Tableware had 128 Herend bunny and rabbit figurines in stock. Most come in several fishnet colors, from Blue and Raspberry to Chocolate, and 17 are made in a single decoration. Choose the pose first, then the color.

Which colors do Herend bunnies come in?

Blue leads by a wide margin, followed by Raspberry, which is Herend's name for its pink fishnet. Chocolate is offered least among the solid colors.

Looking for a pink bunny? Choose Raspberry, or the Herend Polka Dot Rabbit Figurine, which is white with pink dots. For blue, Sapphire is a deeper shade than Blue. All-white bunnies are rare: only the Herend Lop Ear Bunny Figurine and the Herend Scratching Bunny Figurine list White, and a few models such as the Herend Blossom Bunny Figurine pair white with a fishnet color.

Bunny or rabbit: what the names tell you

Of the 128 pieces, 101 have Bunny or Bunnies in the name, 22 say Rabbit, and one is a hare, the Herend Snowshoe Hare Figurine. We keep Herend's names exactly as they are.

Across the line-up, the rabbit models tend to show the animal in a natural pose, as in the Herend Lying Rabbit Figurine, the Herend Running Rabbit Figurine and the Herend Large Standing Rabbit Figurine. Bunny models usually come with a prop, a costume or a hobby: the Herend Chef Bunny Figurine, the Herend Cello Bunny Figurine, the Herend Cowboy Bunny Figurine. There are exceptions, so treat this as a quick way to scan the list.

Herend bunny sizes, from miniature to showpiece

Four miniatures stand one to two and a quarter inches high. Two of them have similar names but are different models: the Herend Miniature Rabbit Figurine is model 15246, and the Herend Rabbit Miniature Figurine is model 05338.

Ordering a Herend bunny for Easter

Interest in Herend bunnies peaks from February through April. Stock is held color by color, so a model can be available in Blue and sold out in Green. If one color matters, order early or email info@paristableware.com and we will check.

Caring for a porcelain bunny

Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. Lift a bunny by its body, not its ears. Our care and replacement guide has the rest.

Common questions

Are Herend bunnies hand painted?

Yes. Herend figurines are painted by hand at the manufactory in Herend, Hungary, with squirrel-hair brushes and real gold, according to Herend's own description of its craft. That is why two bunnies of the same model and color can differ slightly.

What is the most popular color for a Herend bunny?

We can speak for our own stock: on October 24, 2026, Blue was offered on 94 of the 128 bunny and rabbit models we carried, and Raspberry, Herend's pink, on 87. Chocolate was the least offered solid color, on 32.

Can I match a Herend bunny I already own?

Look for the five-digit model number in the item number; it stays the same in every color. Our Herend figurines guide explains how to read the full item number, and with it you can look up the same model at Paris Tableware.

What is a pink Herend bunny called?

Herend calls its pink fishnet Raspberry. For a softer look, the Herend Polka Dot Rabbit Figurine is white with pink dots.

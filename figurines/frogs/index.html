Do Herend frogs come in colors other than green?

Yes. Green and Key Lime dominate, but Raspberry and Blue are each offered on 5 frog models, Sapphire and Butterscotch on 2, and Rust on the Herend Frog with Crown Figurine. A pink frog makes a light-hearted change from the expected green, and the two-color pairs mix both.

Herend frog gifts

  • For a yoga teacher or student: the three yoga frogs. The Tree Pose model balances on one leg on a lily pad.
  • For a wedding or engagement: the Herend Frog Prince Figurine, wearing a tall gold crown, for anyone who has kissed a few frogs. In our photo the Herend Frog with Crown Figurine wears a smaller crown and holds a gold ball.
  • For a gardener: the Herend Frog with Ladybug Figurine, the Herend Flower Frog Figurine or the Herend Frog on Lily Pad Figurine.
  • For new parents: the Herend Mother and Baby Frog Figurine, with the baby riding on its mother's back.

Frogs share the garden theme with the ladybugs, butterflies, snails and bees in the store's Herend bugs and friends collection.

Caring for a frog figurine

Lift a frog by its body rather than a crown or a raised leg. Dust with a soft cloth, and skip abrasives and lemon-based products. Our care and replacement guide has more.

Common questions

What is the Herend Frog Prince?

The Herend Frog Prince Figurine is a fishnet frog wearing a tall gold crown, 3.25 inches high, offered in Green, Blue and Key Lime on October 24, 2026. It suits a wedding or engagement gift.

Do Herend frogs come in colors other than green?

Yes. Raspberry and Blue were each offered on 5 frog models on October 24, 2026, Sapphire and Butterscotch on 2, and Rust on the Herend Frog with Crown Figurine. Two-color pairs such as Key Lime-Raspberry mix green with another color.

How many Herend yoga frogs are there?

We carried three on October 24, 2026: the Herend Yoga Frog Figurine, the Herend Yoga Frog in Tree Pose Figurine and the Herend Yoga Frog in Cobra Pose Figurine.

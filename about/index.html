Who Runs This Guide

This Herend guide is written and run by Paris Tableware, a family-owned store in Ridgewood, New Jersey, since 1982. We sell Herend figurines, dinnerware and gifts online at paristableware.com. The guide is independent and separate from Herend's own website.

Who we are

Paris Tableware is a family business in Ridgewood, New Jersey. It carries fine European porcelain, crystal and gifts, Herend among them, and ships orders within the continental United States. Paris Tableware is an authorized Herend retailer, as the answer on authorized retailers in the Paris Tableware FAQ confirms. You can reach us at info@paristableware.com or (201) 652-4233.

Is this Herend's website?

No. This is an independent guide written by a store that sells Herend. Herend porcelain is made by the Herend Porcelain Manufactory, founded in 1826 in the village of Herend, Hungary, and its own site is herend.com. Every page here is written by our store.

What this guide covers

The guide is for people who collect Herend figurines or are about to start. It shows which Herend animal figurine to start with, how to read the marks on the base of a piece, where Herend comes from in our history timeline, and how to care for a figurine and replace a broken one. Every piece shown is handmade in Herend, Hungary. For plates and cups, see Herend dinnerware at Paris Tableware.

How we write and check these pages

  • History and craft facts come from named sources, mainly the Herend Porcelain Manufactory (herend.com) and Wikipedia, with collector references for older marks. Each page lists its sources and the date they were checked.
  • Product and pattern names are copied exactly from the Paris Tableware catalog, spelling and accents included.
  • Prices and stock change often, so they appear only on paristableware.com. Each guide links to the matching store page.
  • We review every page each season, including its links to the store.
  • If you spot a mistake, email info@paristableware.com. We will correct the page and update its date.

Who writes the guides

The guides are written by Tamar Medzadourian of Paris Tableware in Ridgewood, who also signs the posts on the Paris Tableware blog.

How this guide is paid for

Paris Tableware pays for this site. Links to paristableware.com lead to our own store, and we earn from purchases made there. Brands and advertisers do not pay for anything on these pages, and we do not publish customer reviews here.

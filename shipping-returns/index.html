Shipping and Returns

The Herend pieces in this guide are sold and shipped by Paris Tableware from Ridgewood, New Jersey. Shipping is free on orders over $150 and a flat $19 on orders of $150 or less, to the continental United States only. Orders placed through January 1, 2027 can be returned within 30 days of delivery; after that our standard is 14 days.

You place and pay for every order at paristableware.com, and the store handles packing, delivery and returns. The same terms apply to every order there, whatever the brand.

How long does a Herend order take to ship?

The store says orders typically ship within 3 to 5 business days. A piece coming from the manufacturer, or a special order, can take longer. If you need a Herend gift by a certain date, email info@paristableware.com or call (201) 652-4233 before ordering and the store will confirm timing.

What if a Herend piece arrives broken?

Porcelain is packed by hand in the store and, wherever possible, ships in Herend's own box inside a cushioned outer carton. If a piece arrives chipped, broken or wrong, keep all the packaging, take photos and email info@paristableware.com within 48 hours of delivery. The store arranges a replacement or refund and pays the return shipping.

For a piece that breaks later at home, our guide to caring for Herend figurines and replacing a broken one explains how to identify it and find a match.

How do returns work?

Email info@paristableware.com to request a return before sending anything back. The store replies with instructions, and parcels that arrive without an approved request are not accepted. Pieces must be unused and in their original packaging, with proof of purchase. Special orders, custom items and sale items cannot be returned.

The store recommends a trackable, insured service for return parcels, because it cannot take responsibility for a return lost or damaged on the way back.

Common questions

Can Paris Tableware ship Herend to Alaska or Hawaii?

No. The store ships only to the 48 contiguous states and Washington, D.C. It cannot ship to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territories, APO/FPO addresses or other countries.

Is shipping free on Herend orders?

Yes, when the order total is over $150. Orders of $150 or less ship for a flat $19. The same rates apply to every brand at paristableware.com.

Who pays to return a Herend piece?

For a change of mind, the customer pays return shipping. Orders placed through January 1, 2027 can be returned within 30 days of delivery; after that our standard is 14 days. When a piece arrives damaged, defective or is the wrong item, the store pays the return shipping.
